# Privacy Policy for Das Radio

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Das Radio is a free live audio streaming application developed by Akash Das. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
Das Radio does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. The app does not require account creation, login, or access to sensitive device sensors.

## 2. Permissions Used
- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: Required to stream audio over the network.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE & FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK**: Required to continue audio playback when the app is in the background or the screen is locked.
- **WAKE_LOCK**: Prevents the streaming processor from sleeping during playback.

## 3. Third-Party Services
Das Radio does not use third-party tracking services or analytics SDKs that identify individual users.

## 4. Children's Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
